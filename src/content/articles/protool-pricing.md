## Which license do you need?

The **Diagnostic license** is the starting point for fault work, live data and documented service functions. Its page says coding is excluded except for battery and injector coding. Check the exact vehicle and module before assuming a named function exists.

The **Coding license** is for ProTool's coding scope. Buying it alone is rational only when diagnostics are not needed or are already covered by another tool.

The **Master license** combines Diagnostic and Coding. Two separate $99.99 licenses total $199.98, so the displayed $174.99 Master price is $24.99 lower by arithmetic. The Master page describes this as a $20 discount, while the displayed individual prices imply a $24.99 difference. Because those figures conflict, verify the current cart or checkout price before purchasing.

For scope rather than price, compare [BimmerCode with ProTool](/tools/bmw-diagnostic-software-comparison/) or [BimmerLink with ProTool](/tools/bmw-diagnostic-software-comparison/) before choosing an entitlement.

The third row assumes all three items are bought separately and the cable returns to stock. The bundle is $19.98 lower than that arithmetic at the checked prices, but its value disappears if you do not need both interfaces. The store page's component-value text also does not perfectly match the individual listings, so verify the actual cart.

An Android phone or tablet is required. If you already own a supported device, its incremental cost is zero. If not, add the device, USB OTG hardware where required, protective power arrangements and any regional tax.

## Adapter choice can change the budget

The official Bluetooth adapter page lists ProTool as well as ISTA, INPA, NCS Expert and Tool32 compatibility. It also states that older E-chassis vehicles can require a double K-line adapter. The Expert K+DCAN page documents wired coverage and options, but was out of stock when checked.

Do not choose an interface solely by wireless convenience. Match the BMW generation, application and job. Coding or programming risk can demand a more controlled path than a quick diagnostic read.

## October 2026 recheck: license eligibility before a price comparison

**Research update: 10 October 2026.** [BimmerGeeks' official ProTool page](https://www.bimmergeeks.net/protool) lists **$99.99 Diagnostic**, **$99.99 Coding**, and **$174.99 Master**. Its [Master purchase page](https://www.bimmergeeks.net/product-page/master-license) explicitly warns that ProTool is available for **Android**, requires an eligible vehicle and does **not** include physical vehicle adapters. The licensing numbers agree with the original September check, but they remain source-dated USD catalog prices, not tax-inclusive, guaranteed checkout totals.

ProTool's own support description states that diagnostic coverage covers OBD2 BMW/MINI/Rolls-Royce models, while coding **excludes older Generation 1 chassis examples such as E46, E39, E38, E53 and E85 and older vehicles**. Those are the *vendor's broad model-family descriptions*, not an individually verified ECU function list. Confirm the precise chassis, controller and required job against the official product help before paying. On a car outside the documented coding scope, a Master license may not make the unsupported coding task possible.

## What each entitlement buys—and does not buy

| Purchase | Vendor-described license boundary | Good purchase question | Still unproven without vehicle checks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Diagnostic ($99.99) | Fault work and diagnostics, with specified battery/injector coding exceptions | Is the requested operation documented in the diagnostic scope? | A named active test or procedure on an exact ECU |
| Coding ($99.99) | Coding features, separate from the complete diagnostic entitlement | Is coding the actual job, and is the chassis in scope? | Support for excluded older families and equipment-dependent features |
| Master ($174.99) | Diagnostic and Coding entitlements together | Do I genuinely need both areas? | That buying a broader license changes vehicle capability |
| Compatible adapter | Hardware, not a software entitlement | Does the intended phone, protocol and car work with this interface? | A Bluetooth label alone cannot verify module access |
| Android phone/tablet | Required host, not bundled in an app license | Can the owner use a supported existing device? | The exact OS/device and connection setup without trying the free app |

The [ProTool Master page](https://www.bimmergeeks.net/product-page/master-license) recommends downloading the app to check platform suitability and confirming vehicle support before purchase. It notes that a compatible adapter can allow browsing the app before buying. That is **pre-purchase research**, not an independent hands-on claim by this publication.

The $49.99 Bluetooth and $69.99 K+DCAN entries are the **original September 1 seller observations**; they are *not* independently reconfirmed inventory or current cart totals in this update. The wired cable was marked out of stock at that earlier check. The three-year column assumes no renewal charge on these listed one-time licenses, the same Android device and hardware continuing to work, and no additional purchases. Do not count a new phone, accessories, repair, shipping, tax or license-policy change as zero merely because the example excludes them.

The two separate $99.99 software licenses add to **$199.98**, so Master at $174.99 is **$24.99 less by arithmetic**. BimmerGeeks' Master page describes a “$20 discount,” which does not reconcile exactly with those line-item prices. That discrepancy is preserved and flagged rather than smoothed away. The actual basket and any currently available promotions control the final purchase.

## Interface cost is a capability constraint, not an accessory upsell

Before selecting the Bluetooth or wired route, determine whether the exact vehicle is an E-, F-, G- or other platform, what controller needs access, and what transport the **official ProTool support material** documents for that task. The old [adapter comparison hub](/tools/bmw-obd-adapter-comparison/) and [BMW interface architecture map](/guides/bmw-diagnostic-interface-map/) explain why K+DCAN, ENET and wireless OBD cannot be treated as interchangeable connectors. They are research starting points; ProTool eligibility should still be confirmed in its own documentation.

| Configuration concern | What to check | Purchase consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Older chassis with restricted coding | Vendor's exclusions and the intended ECU | A Master license may not remedy the restriction |
| An Android device already owned | Device/OS support and ProTool installation | Avoid adding a duplicate phone cost |
| Wireless convenience versus stable connection | Official protocol and function-specific interface support | The cheapest adapter may be unsuitable |
| Need for both wired and wireless adapters | Identify two actual jobs that require the paths | The multi-item bundle may otherwise be unnecessary |
| Battery/injector exception | Confirm the precise documented diagnostic-license exception for your module | Do not automatically buy Coding to obtain a task already in scope |
| Active calibration, programming or repair | Qualified repair guidance, prerequisites and security authorization | App purchase does not confer safe procedure or authorization |

This site does not advise running unverified controller resets or vehicle programming merely to “test” a license. Read-only verification and documentation are safer starting points; high-risk procedures require the relevant qualified service process.

## The cheapest defensible route

1. List the exact tasks and control units.
2. Choose Diagnostic, Coding or Master from that scope.
3. Check whether an existing Android device and interface are officially supported.
4. Add only the missing interface path.
5. Recheck price, stock, tax, shipping and return terms at checkout.

For adjacent budgets, the [BimmerCode pricing guide](/guides/bimmercode-pricing/) and [BimmerLink pricing guide](/guides/bimmerlink-pricing/) use the same app-plus-adapter accounting boundary.

## Bottom line

The realistic entry point is **$149.98 before tax and shipping** for the checked Diagnostic-plus-Bluetooth setup when Android hardware is already owned. A broader wireless setup is **$224.98** with Master plus Bluetooth. Those totals are snapshots, not permanent MSRP. The best budget is the smallest verified license-and-interface combination that covers the exact BMW job.
