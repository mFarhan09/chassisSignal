**Choose ProTool for a BMW-focused Android workflow and a one-time license matched to diagnostics, coding or both. Choose Carly when its vehicle checker confirms the functions you need and its subscription package, included scanner and broader brand plan suit you.** Compare the same tasks over the same ownership period.

## What you buy with ProTool

BimmerGeeks currently lists Diagnostic and Coding licenses at **$99.99 each** and the Master license at **$174.99**, checked September 1, 2026. The Master license combines the two scopes. An Android device and supported adapter remain part of the working setup unless already owned or included in a separate bundle.

ProTool's diagnostic description covers fault, live-data and service work while reserving most coding for the Coding entitlement. Vehicle and module coverage still needs verification; a license name is not a universal ECU list. The [BimmerCode versus ProTool guide](/guides/bimmercode-vs-protool/) compares their coding focus directly.

## What you buy with Carly

Carly's current [Premium Package explanation](https://support.mycarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/20084641053586-What-is-Carly-Premium-Package) describes a subscription license and a free universal scanner. It also says price depends on selected brand coverage, add-ons, location and currency and is shown on the website. That makes a single global dollar figure misleading.

Carly's [supported-feature guidance](https://support.mycarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011226299-What-features-does-Carly-support-for-my-car) is essential: diagnostics, live data, battery or service functions and coding vary by model and build year. BMW coding requires Carly's scanner and license, and available options remain vehicle-specific.

The [Carly subscription cost guide](/guides/carly-subscription-cost/) explains how to record renewal terms, region and add-ons. For a coding-first alternative, see [BimmerCode versus Carly](/guides/bimmercode-vs-carly/).

## Compare three-year ownership, not just checkout

For ProTool, total the selected license, adapter and any Android hardware. For Carly, total the displayed subscription over the period you expect to keep it, confirm renewal terms and include paid add-ons. Do not value an included adapter as a saving if it will not serve another required app.

No universal winner can be calculated without a vehicle, feature list, country, currency and time horizon. A transparent spreadsheet with those inputs is more reliable than a headline “cheaper” claim.

## Function depth and recovery

Both vendors document BMW functions, but labels such as diagnostics or coding are too broad for purchase. Check the exact control unit and desired operation. Carly documents coding backups and restoration in supported cases; that does not guarantee recovery from every interruption or unsupported change. ProTool's scope likewise must be tied to the vehicle and module.

## Who should choose which?

- **ProTool:** BMW-focused owner or independent technician, Android already available, supported interface confirmed, one-time entitlement preferred.
- **Carly:** buyer who values the guided consumer workflow, included scanner, supported multi-brand plan or iOS use, and accepts recurring cost.
- **Neither yet:** buyer who has not verified the exact vehicle, module and function or who needs factory programming rather than app-level diagnosis/coding.

## Bottom line

ProTool versus Carly is mainly a platform, entitlement and ownership-model decision. Verify exact functions first; then compare complete cost over a fixed period. ProTool's one-time BMW focus and Carly's subscription-led package can each be sensible, but only after the vehicle checker—not the marketing category—confirms the job.
