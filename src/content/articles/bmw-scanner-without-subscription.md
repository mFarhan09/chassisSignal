> **Research status:** Pricing and update policies change. Verify the current vendor terms before purchase.

## Key findings

“No subscription” can mean several things: core functions continue without payment, updates are optional, a mobile app has a one-time license, or only some services recur. Compare the entire ownership model.

## Four cost layers

1. Hardware purchase
2. Included vehicle software or app access
3. Optional or required updates
4. Recurring cloud, report or premium services

## What to ask

Determine what still works if no further payment is made. Then ask whether new vehicle coverage, bug fixes or operating-system compatibility depend on paid updates.

## Comparison worksheet

| Cost item | At purchase | After year one |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware | Record current price | Usually retained |
| Vehicle package | Confirm what is included | Confirm continued access |
| Updates | Included or optional | Record policy |
| App services | One-time or recurring | Record renewal terms |
