For a current BMW shortlist, compare exact Autel model numbers—not “the 808” against “the 900.” Buyers comparing another broad tablet family can use the [Launch X431 for BMW guide](/guides/launch-x431-bmw/) to apply the same exact-model discipline. The MaxiCOM MK808S is a wired 7-inch all-system tablet with active tests and service functions. The MaxiCOM MK900 is a newer wired 8-inch all-system tablet that adds documented pre/post scan, DoIP, and CAN FD support. Bluetooth and TPMS suffixes identify materially different variants.

Choose the MK808S when it proves every BMW module and function you need and wired communication is acceptable. Move to the MK900 when a target BMW requires its newer communication protocols, when pre/post reporting matters, or when the larger current platform better fits the job. Do not borrow a feature from MK808BT Pro, MK808S-TS, MK900-BT, or MK900-TS and assign it to the base model.

## Freeze the model names before comparing

Autel maintains closely related product lines with small suffix changes. Those suffixes can change the vehicle communication interface, TPMS hardware, battery-analysis support, cable arrangement, and price. A reseller headline may shorten several products to “MK808” or “MK900,” which makes a clean-looking table technically unreliable.

The broader [OBD app versus handheld scanner guide](/guides/obd-app-vs-handheld-scanner/) helps decide whether a dedicated tablet is the right ownership architecture at all. For this guide, the two anchors are:

- **MaxiCOM MK808S:** current 7-inch wired model in Autel's MK808S/MK808Z documentation.
- **MaxiCOM MK900:** current 8-inch wired model in Autel's MK900 documentation.

The Bluetooth and TS variants remain separate. A cart should preserve the complete model suffix from product page to receipt. For a direct exact-SKU cross-vendor decision, compare the [Foxwell NT530 Plus with the wired Autel MK808S](/guides/foxwell-nt530-vs-autel-mk808s-bmw/).

## MK808S: the documented baseline

Autel describes the MK808S as an Android 11 tablet with a 7-inch 1024×600 display, 4 GB of memory, 64 GB of storage, and wired vehicle communication. Its current comparison table lists code reading and erasing, live data, ECU information, active tests, service functions, complete OBD diagnostics, record storage, and several named maintenance functions.

Those platform claims make it a plausible BMW owner or light-workshop tool. They do not establish every BMW service function. The accompanying manual says it can read and clear codes for available modules on the majority of supported makes and lists examples such as oil, EPB, [steering-angle calibration](/guides/bmw-steering-angle-sensor-calibration-tool/), battery-management, DPF, and [TPMS-related functions](/guides/bmw-tpms-diagnostic-tool/). “Available” and “majority” are important limits. For the qualification checklist to prove actuator commands before buying, see our [BMW bidirectional scan tool guide](/guides/bmw-bidirectional-scan-tool-functions/).

The same Autel table distinguishes the MK808S from MK808BT Pro and MK808S-TS. Wireless communication and complete TPMS functions belong to specific variants. Do not assume the base MK808S includes them.

## MK900: newer protocols and reporting

Autel describes the MK900 as an 8-inch Android 11 all-system tablet. The current page lists code functions, live data, active tests, service functions, AutoVIN, pre/post scan reporting, DoIP, and CAN FD. Its base model uses wired vehicle communication; MK900-BT and MK900-TS are separate variants.

DoIP and CAN FD can matter on newer vehicle architectures, but protocol support is not a blanket promise of BMW function coverage. It establishes that the hardware and platform advertise those communication paths. The exact vehicle software still determines the menus available after identification.

Pre/post scan can be valuable when a workshop needs a before-and-after record. An individual owner who needs one confirmed battery or brake function may not benefit enough to justify a larger platform. Buy the documented capability, not the newer number.

## BMW coverage must resolve below the brand

The [BMW code reader versus scan tool guide](/guides/bmw-code-reader-vs-scan-tool/) defines the capability ladder behind this check. Autel manuals show why “BMW supported” is only a first gate. A current MaxiCheck MX900 manual illustrates a BMW scan with ACSM and ABS-DSC as distinct control units. Other menus appear after the vehicle is identified, and available functions vary.

Build one row per required BMW outcome:

1. exact model, chassis, build month/year, engine, and region;
2. target module;
3. read codes and save report;
4. required live-data item;
5. required service function;
6. required active command;
7. protocol or gateway condition;
8. any online access, account, or authorization.

Use Autel's coverage resources or support to resolve those rows for the exact scanner. A successful AutoVIN claim does not prove the target function; manual vehicle selection may still be necessary, and the function can remain absent. For module-specific examples, see how to [verify BMW transfer-case coverage](/guides/bmw-transfer-case-adaptation-reset-tool/) and [verify injector coding for the exact engine](/guides/bmw-injector-coding-tool/).

Treat every active command as its own pass/fail requirement instead of transferring a platform-level claim. Keep the evidence with the exact scanner suffix and software date.

## Updates and post-expiry behavior

Autel states that tablet scanners include a period of software updates and offers renewal through the tablet, the Autel portal, an authorized dealer, or an update card. Its store guidance also says a scanner can continue to perform existing basic diagnostics after the included term ends, while new coverage and some online or advanced functions may require current access.

Translate that into five cart questions:

- What is the included update duration for this exact SKU and region?
- On what date does the term begin?
- Which installed functions continue after expiry?
- Which online, protected, or newly added functions stop or require renewal?
- What is the current renewal price for the exact serial/model class?

Do not copy a renewal price from another Autel series. The support FAQ lists many diagnostic families and paths, which is precisely why the model must stay attached to the entitlement.

## When a different Autel variant makes sense

Use the family map to keep the decision compact:

| Need | Current family direction | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Base wired tablet | MK808S or MK900 | Choose by exact BMW function and protocol requirement |
| Wireless vehicle link | MK808BT Pro or MK900-BT | Confirm the included VCI; Bluetooth is not implied by the base name |
| Full TPMS workflow | MK808S-TS or MK900-TS | Complete sensor activation/programming belongs to the TS variant evidence |
| Requirement not resolved here | Research the 906, 908, or MaxiSYS family separately | A higher family name is not automatic proof of the missing BMW function |

Autel’s current product index separates these families, so the suffix and series are part of the technical specification. Step outside the 808/900 comparison only when a named requirement demands it, then rebuild the same vehicle-and-function matrix for that exact model.

For a clean coding-versus-diagnostics boundary, the [BimmerCode versus Foxwell NT530 guide](/guides/bimmercode-vs-foxwell-nt530/) shows why a coding app and a diagnostic platform should not be treated as substitutes.

Do not choose a suffix because it sounds newer. Confirm what is in the case, how it connects, whether the base tablet functions differ, and which accessories or functions depend on the variant.

## Final selection

Choose the **MK808S** when its exact BMW coverage passes, wired operation is acceptable, and you do not need the MK900's documented DoIP, integrated CAN FD, or pre/post scan additions. Choose the **MK900** when one of those additions is required or when its current platform and larger display justify the difference.

If the required function remains unresolved, neither model has passed. For ABS or restraint intent, preserve the module-access and safety boundaries described above. The most expensive tablet still cannot replace exact vehicle coverage, current service information, or a competent repair process.
