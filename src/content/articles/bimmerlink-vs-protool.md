BimmerLink and ProTool overlap in BMW diagnostics, yet they are built around different scopes. BimmerLink is a focused app for iOS and Android with fault access, live data and selected service functions. ProTool runs on Android and divides its BMW toolset into separately purchased diagnostic and coding scopes.

The choice starts with platform and job, not with the size of the menu.

## Quick recommendation

Choose BimmerLink when iOS is required or when the task is a documented scan, live value or listed service function. Choose ProTool Diagnostic when Android is acceptable and the job needs its published broader diagnostic, calibration or logging scope. Add ProTool's Coding or Master license only when documented coding work is actually required.

## Scope comparison

| Boundary | BimmerLink | ProTool |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Platform | iOS and Android | Android |
| Product model | Focused diagnostic/service app | Modular toolset |
| Fault access | Published across supported control units | Published all-module diagnostic scope |
| Live data | Display and CSV logging | Live data and logging in Diagnostic |
| Service work | Listed functions by supported vehicle | Broader published tests, resets and calibrations |
| Coding | Not its product role | Separate Coding or Master license |
| Adapter | Vehicle-aware approved list | Publisher-supported interface |

This does not score “more” as automatically better. Broader scope increases both value and operator responsibility.

## Platform is a hard gate

If the maintained device is iOS, BimmerLink remains a candidate and ProTool does not. ProTool's publisher describes Android only. Buying another device may be rational for a wider toolset, but include that hardware and management burden in cost.

On Android, compare the actual tasks. A simple fault scan does not become more accurate merely because an app also sells coding. Conversely, a workflow needing ProTool's documented calibration or expert diagnostics cannot be evaluated only from BimmerLink's approachable interface.

## Service functions require exact qualification

BimmerLink lists functions such as battery registration, DPF regeneration, parking-brake service mode and service reset for supported cars. ProTool Diagnostic publishes tests, resets, calibrations and initialization scope. Neither list is a universal vehicle promise.

Confirm chassis, build date, relevant control unit, app version and adapter. Distinguish a menu entry from a verified procedure. A service function can have physical prerequisites and safety consequences that the button label does not convey.

## Price the needed license boundary

At verification, ProTool listed Diagnostic and Coding licenses at $99.99 each and Master at $174.99. These are date-sensitive storefront prices. BimmerLink pricing belongs to the current platform store and may be separate by ecosystem.

Add the approved adapter and, for ProTool, any Android hardware needed. Do not buy Master merely because its unit cost is lower than two licenses unless both scopes have a real job.

The [BimmerCode pricing guide](/guides/bimmercode-pricing/) covers that separate app's platform costs, and [BimmerCode versus ProTool](/guides/bimmercode-vs-protool/) owns the coding-led comparison.

## Diagnostic workflow

Whichever app is selected:

1. capture vehicle identity and the original full scan;
2. preserve freeze-frame or contextual data available in the tool;
3. investigate the system before clearing evidence;
4. use live values only with a hypothesis and documented range;
5. perform service functions only under current vehicle/app instructions;
6. rescan and record the outcome.

This package does not claim hands-on testing and intentionally excludes step-by-step coding or safety-critical service procedures.

## Who should buy which?

**BimmerLink:** owners and technicians wanting a portable focused path on iOS or Android for its documented diagnostics and service functions.

**ProTool Diagnostic:** Android users who can use the broader diagnostic surface and understand its advanced operations.

**ProTool Master:** users with a verified need for both diagnostic and coding scopes, not buyers seeking a universal safety shortcut.

**Neither alone:** work requiring BMW guided test plans, repair information, programming infrastructure or specialist recovery.

## Support and maintenance fit

Also compare the support path around the software. Record the app version, Android or iOS release, adapter firmware and vehicle identity before opening a case. A focused product can be easier to support because its boundary is smaller; a broad toolset can be easier for an experienced user who keeps one documented environment. Neither advantage survives an unapproved adapter or an unsupported vehicle combination.

## Bottom line

BimmerLink wins on focused cross-platform access. ProTool wins when an Android workflow genuinely needs its broader licensed diagnostic or coding surface. Let platform eliminate impossible choices, let the exact task set the scope, and let current vehicle/adapter documentation make the final compatibility decision.
