## Direct answer: BimmerCode vs Carly

**The direct answer:** BimmerCode and Carly overlap on supported coding, but they are not exact like-for-like products. Based on current official documentation, BimmerCode is the more focused coding route. Carly documents a broader yearly-license ecosystem that can include supported diagnostics, coding, live data, maintenance, battery functions, and other ownership tools.

That does not make Carly the automatic choice. If you mainly want occasional coding and your exact car, module, platform, and adapter are supported, BimmerCode may be the more direct route. If you want coding plus diagnostics and maintenance functions within one paid ecosystem, Carly may fit better—provided those exact functions are available for your vehicle.

There is also a third comparison owners often miss: **BimmerCode and BimmerLink are separate products**. BimmerLink, not BimmerCode, is the developer's product for documented functions such as fault-code diagnostics, live sensor data, battery registration, DPF functions, and service functions. An owner who wants BimmerCode's coding approach plus those capabilities may need to compare **BimmerCode + BimmerLink** against Carly.

If battery registration is your main requirement, see our [BMW battery-registration scanner guide](/guides/bmw-battery-registration-scanner/) for tool and compatibility checks.

> **Testing disclosure:** Chassis Signal has not physically tested BimmerCode or Carly for this comparison. This article is based on current official compatibility, support, pricing, licensing, and platform documentation.

## Why this is not a simple feature-for-feature comparison

A row-by-row feature count hides the most important difference. [BimmerCode describes itself around coding and customization](https://bimmercode.app/), while its [FAQ separates BimmerLink's monitoring and diagnostics role](https://bimmercode.app/faq/). Carly's current [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/carly-obd2-car-scanner/id467344155) and [feature support documentation](https://support.mycarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360011226299-What-features-does-Carly-support-for-my-car) cover more ownership categories inside its paid ecosystem.

The products therefore overlap in one important area—supported coding—but differ in documented scope, hardware path, licensing, and recurring-cost structure. Calling them **partial substitutes** is Chassis Signal analysis based on those official product-scope facts, not wording from either company.

## Decide what job you actually need the tool to do

Start with the job, not the brand:

- **Coding and customization:** compare the exact coding options each app exposes for your car.
- **Fault diagnostics or live data:** compare Carly with BimmerLink, or with a combined BimmerCode + BimmerLink route—not BimmerCode alone.
- **Battery registration, DPF, or service functions:** verify the exact vehicle and function. These are BimmerLink functions in SG Software's product line and documented Carly categories where supported.
- **One paid ownership ecosystem:** Carly may align more closely with this goal, but the category list is not a promise that every function works on every car.
- **Minimal recurring software cost:** the verified BimmerCode US iOS purchase does not document a recurring subscription, while Carly Premium is yearly. Hardware and any separate BimmerLink purchase still belong in the comparison.
- **Reuse hardware you already own:** count that advantage only if the adapter is officially listed for the chosen app, exact vehicle/series, and platform.

This framing prevents a common mistake: buying for a broad label such as “G-series compatible” or “BMW diagnostics” before confirming the exact requested function.

### Compatibility is an intersection, not a badge

Chassis Signal's decision framework treats useful support as the intersection of:

**vehicle/model year ∩ installed ECU/module ∩ adapter ∩ phone/platform ∩ paid entitlement ∩ requested function**

Every applicable layer must align. If one layer is undocumented, the responsible result is **unknown**, not “probably supported.”

## Coding capability

BimmerCode says available coding options depend on factors including software, vehicle configuration, optional equipment, and installed control units. Its [Quick Start Guide](https://bimmercode.app/manual/) says the free version can be used with a supported adapter to inspect available control units and coding options before buying the full version. That is a useful availability check, not a performance test or guarantee that a desired option will appear.

Carly also documents BMW coding, but its [coding support](https://support.mycarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/20233353072786-Which-coding-options-can-I-use-on-my-BMW) and [missing-coding guidance](https://support.mycarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010757879-Why-can-t-I-see-my-desired-coding) qualify availability by model, year, installed ECU functions, and app support. Carly's Lite view may show possible coding options, but the displayed set still depends on the actual car.

Neither documentation set supports a blanket conclusion about which app has deeper coding, completes a job faster, or behaves more reliably. CS-001 has no controlled hands-on testing.

Safety language also needs restraint. BimmerCode documents backups and preventive module blocks, but its FAQ also qualifies expert-mode mistakes and module states that may require reflash. Carly documents an FRM risk check and warning; Chassis Signal does not recommend bypassing it. This comparison does not provide coding, recovery, or override procedures.

## Diagnostics and live data: keep BimmerLink separate

The cleanest way to avoid an inaccurate comparison is to give BimmerLink its own column and price.

[BimmerLink's official product page](https://bimmerlink.app/) documents reading and clearing trouble codes, real-time sensor values, logging, battery registration, DPF functions, parking-brake service mode, and service reset where supported. Those are not BimmerCode functions merely because the same developer sells both apps.

Carly distinguishes free/basic generic OBD from its paid manufacturer-specific diagnostics. Its [diagnostics support page](https://support.mycarly.com/hc/en-us/articles/19922068541074-What-should-I-do-if-Diagnostics-doesn-t-work-for-my-car) says the exact car and license affect whether manufacturer-specific control-unit diagnostics are available; an unsupported or unlicensed configuration may fall back to Basic OBD.

For an owner who wants both coding and diagnostics, compare these actual routes:

1. BimmerCode alone for supported coding.
2. BimmerCode plus the separately purchased BimmerLink for supported diagnostics/service functions.
3. Carly's yearly paid ecosystem for the supported functions included in the chosen entitlement.

## Subscription and account differences

BimmerCode purchases are platform-specific. Its FAQ says purchases do not transfer between iOS and Android, while restoration on the same platform/account is documented; native desktop licensing is machine-specific.

Carly documents a yearly Premium entitlement and account access across supported iOS and Android devices using the same login/email. Billing management depends on purchase channel:

- Website renewal cancellation requires contacting Carly support at least one month before renewal.
- Apple subscriptions are managed through Apple; the current listing states cancellation at least 24 hours before the period ends.
- Google Play subscriptions are managed through Google Play.
- Deleting a Carly account does not itself cancel automatic renewal.
- Refund and all-brand upgrade routes also differ by channel.

This is why “subscription versus one-time purchase” is only part of the decision. Platform transfer, hardware, separate products, and the buyer's needed functions still matter.

## BimmerCode vs Carly: which should you choose?

There is no responsible universal winner.

**Choose the BimmerCode route for consideration first** when coding is the main job, the desired option appears for the exact car/module, and a listed adapter works with the chosen platform. This is a focused-route recommendation, not a claim that BimmerCode has better coding depth or performance.

**Consider Carly first** when the goal is supported diagnostics, coding, live data, and maintenance functions inside one yearly paid ecosystem. Verify the exact car and function before treating the broader category list as useful coverage.

**Compare BimmerCode + BimmerLink against Carly** when you prefer BimmerCode for coding but also need diagnostics or service functions. Keep the products, licenses, prices, and compatibility checks separate.

**For subscription-sensitive buyers**, compare Carly's yearly entitlement with the verified non-recurring BimmerCode US iOS purchase—but include adapter hardware and any required BimmerLink purchase. A one-line app price does not settle the cost.

**For owners with existing hardware**, count a savings only after confirming that exact adapter is officially supported for the app, vehicle/series, module, and phone/platform. Physical OBD fit is insufficient.

## What the documentation does not let us conclude

The approved evidence does not establish that:

- either product is faster, more stable, more reliable, safer, or deeper at coding;
- every BMW or MINI inside a broad support range gets every advertised function;
- any OBD adapter works with both ecosystems;
- the Carly Universal Scanner works with BimmerCode;
- Veepeak BLE/BLE+ universally supports BimmerCode on G-series;
- Carly has one universal price;
- BimmerCode is always cheaper.

Documentation can also contain tension that should remain visible. Broad vehicle language and exact adapter/module tables do not always share the same cutoff. BimmerCode's safety reassurance is qualified by expert-mode and reflash language. Carly's website package and App Store IAPs are different price objects; its two BMW-related US iOS SKU names are not explained; and renewal rules depend on purchase channel.

Unknown means unknown. It is safer to remove a promised capability from the buying case than to fill a gap with assumptions or forum anecdotes.

## Final decision checklist

Before paying for software or hardware:

1. Write down the exact coding, diagnostic, live-data, battery, or service function you need.
2. Confirm the exact vehicle, model year, and installed ECU/module.
3. Check that the adapter is officially listed for the app and exact vehicle/series.
4. Confirm the selected adapter and app support your phone or desktop platform.
5. Confirm whether the function needs BimmerCode, the separate BimmerLink product, or a specific Carly entitlement.
6. Separate hardware, initial software, recurring entitlement, and optional software.
7. Treat every undocumented layer as unknown.
8. Recheck compatibility, prices, and billing terms immediately before purchase.

That process is less satisfying than a one-word winner, but it is much more likely to produce a toolchain that fits the car and the job.
