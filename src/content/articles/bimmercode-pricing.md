BimmerCode is a free download with an in-app purchase for the Full Version, plus a separate compatible OBD adapter. On the US Apple App Store, the **BimmerCode Full Version is $49.99** as of August 27, 2026. That is a storefront-specific observation in US dollars, not a universal global price.

Android uses its own Google Play in-app purchase. BimmerCode says licenses are platform-specific: an iOS purchase does not transfer to Android, and vice versa. Check the price inside the intended regional storefront before buying.

## What the Full Version purchase means

Apple lists the app itself as free with in-app purchases and currently shows the Full Version at $49.99 in the US storefront. Google Play labels the Android app as containing in-app purchases but may not expose the buyer's local amount on the public web listing. Currency, tax and store pricing can vary by country and account.

BimmerCode's FAQ says the Full Version can be used on multiple devices on the same platform without paying again, using the account that made the purchase. iOS restoration uses the same Apple ID and Restore Purchases; Android restoration uses the same Google account. A move between iOS and Android requires another license.

The FAQ also identifies a desktop license tied to the machine. Treat desktop as a separate purchase path and verify its current price and compatibility in the app rather than inferring it from mobile.

## The ownership-cost formula

Use:

**BimmerCode ownership cost = platform Full Version + compatible adapter + tax/shipping + any second-platform license**

| Layer | What to capture | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App | Storefront, Full Version price, currency and date | Prices are regional and can change |
| Platform | iOS, Android or desktop | Licenses do not transfer across platforms |
| Adapter | Exact model, seller, tax and shipping | The app requires supported hardware |
| Vehicle | Model, generation, year and intended option | Support is vehicle/function specific |
| Device | Supported OS/device and account | Determines connection and purchase restoration |

Do not add BimmerLink to the BimmerCode price automatically. It is a different app with diagnostic/service functions and its own purchase. Buy it only if its documented job is required.

## Check compatibility before price

BimmerCode tells buyers to check vehicle compatibility before purchasing the Full Version. Its cars page lists supported models and coding options, while the adapter page maps compatible interfaces. A vehicle family being listed does not mean every option is available on every year, control unit or software level.

Use this order:

1. Find the exact vehicle and desired options on the supported-cars page.
2. Select iOS, Android or desktop.
3. Choose an adapter supported for that vehicle/platform combination.
4. Review the in-app Full Version price in the regional storefront.
5. Preserve the receipt and account used for purchase restoration.

## Example budget without a fake global total

For a US iPhone buyer, the software line is currently $49.99 before applicable tax. Add the current price, tax and shipping of one adapter from BimmerCode's supported list. That produces a dated buyer-specific total.

For an Android buyer, record the in-app Google Play amount visible to that account and region, then add supported hardware. For a household that needs both platforms, budget two platform licenses because the purchase cannot be transferred.

No universal total is published here because adapter choice, seller, location, storefront and taxes change the result.

## Safety boundary

Use only documented coding options for the exact vehicle. Maintain stable power, follow the official workflow and avoid Expert Mode changes you do not understand. The BimmerCode FAQ warns that incorrect Expert Mode use can require a control-unit reflash. This pricing guide does not instruct security bypasses, odometer changes, emissions defeat or unsupported ECU modification.

## Bottom line

Treat BimmerCode as a platform-specific Full Version purchase plus compatible hardware. Verify the exact vehicle and option first, then capture the current local storefront amount, adapter cost, tax and shipping. The US iOS price is a useful dated reference—not a worldwide quote.
