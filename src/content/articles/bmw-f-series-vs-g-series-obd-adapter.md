A BMW F Series vs G Series OBD adapter decision cannot be made from connector shape alone. The safe chain is **exact vehicle → app and task → documented interface → supported adapter → phone platform**. F/G family narrows the lookup; it does not replace it.

## Choose the app role first

BimmerCode is a coding/customization app. Its official BMW pages list supported models and options, and its adapter table maps Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and ENET choices by series and platform. BimmerLink is a diagnostics/service app: it documents control-unit diagnostics, live values, CSV logging and named functions such as battery registration and service reset. Its adapter path directs buyers to the current supported list.

The apps can share adapter families without sharing the same job. A coding result is not evidence of diagnostic/service coverage, and diagnostic coverage is not evidence of a coding option.

## F/G compatibility matrix

| Vehicle/app task | Interface family to investigate | Adapter evidence | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F-series BimmerCode coding | Supported Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or ENET path shown for vehicle/platform | Current adapter table | Confirm exact model, option and phone OS |
| G-series BimmerCode coding | Supported Bluetooth/Wi-Fi or ENET; some newer units require specified adapters | Table plus quick-start note | Do not generalize one G-series result |
| F-series BimmerLink diagnosis/service | Interface and adapter supported by BimmerLink | Official function/vehicle page and adapter list | Verify named function for exact vehicle |
| G-series BimmerLink diagnosis/service | Same evidence chain with exact vehicle/function check | Official app and adapter records | Newer modules can change access needs |

**OBD connector** describes the physical access point. **ENET** describes an Ethernet-based BMW communication path. Bluetooth and Wi-Fi describe wireless connection families, but their labels alone do not establish protocol, app or vehicle support.

## Purchase sequence

1. Record exact BMW model, production year and equipment.
2. Define coding, diagnosis, live data or a named service function.
3. Check that vehicle and task on the official app page.
4. Open the current adapter table and filter by family and phone platform.
5. Confirm Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, ENET cable or ENET wireless path.
6. Recheck before purchase because support can change.

## Safety and bottom line

This is compatibility guidance, not coding, emissions, immobilizer or ECU-modification instruction. Buy from the exact app/vehicle/task record outward. No universal F-adapter or G-adapter claim is supported.
