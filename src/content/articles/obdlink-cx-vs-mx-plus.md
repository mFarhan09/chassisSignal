> **Evidence scope:** Current manufacturer and app documentation should be checked at purchase time. No throughput or range testing is claimed.

## Key findings

The CX and MX+ are better understood through ecosystem fit than through a single hierarchy. Start with the required app, mobile platform and vehicles beyond BMW or MINI.

## Different decision contexts

An owner focused on a supported BMW coding workflow may prioritize a different interface than an owner who also needs broad third-party OBD app compatibility across several makes.

## Comparison questions

| Dimension | What to document |
|---|---|
| App support | Exact app and platform listing |
| Vehicle scope | BMW/MINI only or multi-make use |
| Connection | Supported Bluetooth implementation |
| Firmware | Update method and current release information |

## Avoid inferred compatibility

Support for one OBDLink model in an app should not be transferred to another without explicit documentation. Product-family similarity is not evidence of identical support.
