> **Safety note:** Battery work involves electrical risk and vehicle-specific procedures. Use the correct replacement specification and service information; seek professional help when the required coding or recovery path is unclear.

## Key findings

Battery registration records a replacement event in vehicles designed to use that process. It should not be treated as identical to changing battery type or capacity parameters.

## Registration versus coding

Registration generally concerns the replacement event. Coding may be required when relevant battery characteristics change. The exact workflow is vehicle-specific and should be verified from service information.

## Tool research checklist

- Exact chassis and model-year coverage
- Registration function explicitly documented
- Support for changing battery parameters when required
- Clear prerequisites and confirmation feedback
- Stable power and safe replacement procedure

## Avoid generic assumptions

A scanner’s “battery reset” label may cover different functions across brands and vehicles. Look for BMW-specific documentation rather than relying on a universal icon.
