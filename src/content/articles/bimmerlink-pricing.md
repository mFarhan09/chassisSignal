## What the checkout total actually includes

BimmerLink is a free download with a paid full-version unlock, while the usable total also includes a supported adapter and any optional CarPlay add-on; storefront, market and tax determine the exact checkout total.

## The price answer, with the date attached

As observed in the United States on 2026-08-29, Apple's listing makes BimmerLink free to download and shows a **$39.99 one-time full-version unlock**. It also lists a **$9.99 CarPlay add-on** as a separate in-app purchase. Those are US App Store prices in USD; sales tax can be added according to the account and billing address. The listing does not describe the full-version unlock as a recurring subscription.

Android buyers should read the price inside their own Google Play account. Google can localize currency and tax, so this article does not translate the US iOS number into a supposedly universal Android price.

## The ownership equation

The useful total is:

**First purchase total = full-version unlock + supported adapter + optional add-ons + tax**

**Later-year software total = any newly purchased optional add-ons or future charges explicitly shown by the storefront**

That second line is deliberately not a promise that commercial terms can never change. It records the purchase model visible on the observation date. Recheck the storefront before purchase.

## Where the adapter changes the budget

BimmerLink's own documentation requires a supported Bluetooth or Wi-Fi adapter or cable. If you already own one that the current selector supports for your car and phone, the adapter line can be zero. If not, hardware can cost more than the app.

For a concrete US illustration, OBDLink listed CX at **$79.95** on 2026-08-29. Combining that list price with the iOS full-version unlock produces a pre-tax example of **$119.94**. Adding the optional CarPlay item produces **$129.93**. These are examples, not a bundle sold by either company, and shipping or seller discounts can move the hardware line.

## October 2026 entitlement check: diagnostics versus CarPlay

**Research update: 10 October 2026.** The [US Apple listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bimmerlink/id1065360416) continues to show **$39.99** for BimmerLink Full Version and **$9.99** for the optional CarPlay Add-On. These are separate US iOS in-app purchases, not a single $49.98 mandatory app license. BimmerLink's [official description](https://bimmerlink.app/) places fault memories, live readings and selected service functions in the diagnostic product; CarPlay adds an in-car display/control route for eligible features. Exact functions depend on the car and installed equipment.

This page addresses the **cost of owning BimmerLink for a specific task**. It should not duplicate the [broader software-selection matrix](/tools/bmw-diagnostic-software-comparison/) or assert scanner hardware coverage already catalogued in the [adapter comparison](/tools/bmw-obd-adapter-comparison/).

## Which BimmerLink function justifies which payment?

| Intended job | What to budget | What must be verified first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Read and interpret BMW-specific faults | Full Version plus an approved interface if not already owned | Vehicle, connected control units and access to the needed fault data |
| Monitor sensor values on the phone | Full Version and approved interface | Available parameters for the particular ECU; data alone is not a diagnosis |
| Display supported readings via CarPlay | Full Version, **optional CarPlay Add-On**, approved interface | Vehicle's CarPlay setup, supported app functions and legal/safe display use |
| Register a replacement battery | Full Version plus interface **only if eligible** | Battery specification, vehicle module and actual registration support; coding a different capacity/type is a separate issue |
| Enter an electronic parking-brake service mode | Do not buy on marketing wording alone | Exact vehicle/module support and a qualified brake-service procedure |
| Perform a DPF-related function | Only where the diesel vehicle/function is supported | Engine, DPF condition, blockages/faults and thermal safety; professional service when unclear |

The Apple listing describes CarPlay as displaying compatible data and controlling selected exhaust/ASD functions. **It is not necessary merely to connect your phone to read stored faults.** Conversely, buying the $9.99 add-on does not make an unsupported function or control unit accessible. Treat the [service-function eligibility matrix](/tools/bmw-service-function-matrix/) as a cautionary screening resource rather than a promise of app support on every BMW.

## Repeatable one-year and three-year totals

The existing $119.94 example in this guide uses two historical US price observations: a $39.99 iOS Full Version and a $79.95 OBDLink CX list price checked in August. **The adapter observation is historical**; the exact seller, stock and suitability must be rechecked. With unchanged one-time entitlements and no replacements, the cash subtotal is the same at year three; there is no need to invent three annual renewals.

| Scenario with existing iOS device | First-year subtotal | Three-year subtotal if no further purchases | Exclusions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Full Version, already-owned supported interface | $39.99 | $39.99 | Tax; replacement hardware; future changes |
| Full Version plus historical $79.95 adapter example | $119.94 | $119.94 | Tax, shipping, another interface |
| Same example with optional $9.99 CarPlay | $129.93 | $129.93 | Tax, shipping, hardware or entitlement changes |

An Android owner should substitute the actual Google Play purchase and locally available interface price; the US iOS amounts are **not** Android price predictions. If the owner already has a compatible adapter, its *incremental* cash cost can be $0, but only after checking current app/platform/vehicle compatibility. If multiple BMWs share an adapter, validate each vehicle separately rather than assuming the same interface works across generations.

The distinction between cash purchase and annualized cost matters when comparing a licensed phone app with a scanner that charges separately for software updates. BimmerLink's observed US iOS purchase is not listed as an annual subscription. It would be incorrect to count $39.99 three times simply because the comparison horizon is three years. Equally, one should not promise all future vehicle/software support merely because the unlock is currently one-time.

## Full-cost receipt and hardware-selection worksheet

Before paying, capture all six pieces of evidence:

1. **Vehicle:** model, year, installed control unit and precise diagnostic/service operation.
2. **Host:** device, iOS or Android version and the account that will hold the purchase; Apple's listing has its own current operating-system requirements.
3. **Full Version:** displayed price, country, tax and transaction date.
4. **Interface:** official supported adapter/cable for this vehicle and host, actual seller quote, shipping and return conditions.
5. **CarPlay:** explicitly mark yes/no; do not hide the optional purchase inside a mandatory bundle.
6. **Success criterion:** the specific diagnostic information that would answer the question; for a safety-critical service operation, the qualified manufacturer's procedure and stop conditions.

The [BimmerLink official website](https://bimmerlink.app/) and [US App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bimmerlink/id1065360416) explain product scope, but neither substitutes for testing a real user's module. There is no claim of independently testing an OBDLink CX, registering a battery or initiating a vehicle service operation in this review.

## Avoid paying for the wrong BMW software category

BimmerCode and BimmerLink are different purchases with different roles. **BimmerCode** is focused on supported feature coding; **BimmerLink** on fault diagnostics, live data and specified service tasks. Both may require suitable OBD hardware, but shared developer branding does not make one app unlock the other. A handheld BMW scanner may instead package the interface and display together; its hardware price and any update entitlements must be compared on the same first-/third-year basis.

If all you need is a single read-only fault report, first establish which fault data is necessary and whether it is present on the supported vehicle. If your intended action affects brakes, batteries or DPF service, verify the exact function *before* buying and defer physical or active work to the authorized procedure. For a general diagnostic versus coding decision, use the [diagnostic-software comparison](/tools/bmw-diagnostic-software-comparison/); for a new interface, see the [BMW OBD adapter resource](/tools/bmw-obd-adapter-comparison/).

## Choose the spend level before the tool

Buy only the app unlock when you already have a supported interface and have confirmed your exact vehicle. Budget for app plus adapter when this is a first diagnostic setup. Add CarPlay only when its sensor-display workflow matters; it is not needed merely to read faults from the phone.

The comparison with a handheld scanner begins only after defining the job. BimmerLink uses a phone and separate interface. A handheld bundles display, controls and cable into its own hardware. Their sticker prices therefore do not describe the same architecture. When deciding between phone-based diagnostic platforms with one-time or subscription pricing, compare [BimmerLink and Carly](/tools/bmw-diagnostic-software-comparison/) to match the software model to your maintenance routine.

## What to verify at checkout

Record the country storefront, platform, currency, tax display, full-version price, add-on price and exact adapter. Then verify the vehicle on BimmerLink's current support page. This prevents a precise price from creating a false compatibility promise.
