## The garage, not the logo, decides

MX+ is broader in documented networks while BM+ is BMW-application focused.

## Map the whole garage first

A one-BMW purchase and a mixed-brand garage create different answers. List every current vehicle, likely next vehicle, phone or computer platform and required application before comparing adapter prices.

MX+ documents iOS, Android and Windows support plus additional Ford and GM network access. BM+ is positioned as a BMW-focused interface for supported mobile applications. Extra breadth has value only when the garage or software plan will use it.

## Where MX+ reaches beyond the BMW lane

OBDLink documents legislated OBD-II support, multiple host platforms, firmware maintenance and added MS-CAN and SW-CAN access. That makes MX+ the more defensible starting point for a garage that includes supported Ford or GM work as well as general diagnostics.

Broader documented networks do not establish every service function in every app. Match each vehicle and task to the application that will use the adapter.

## Where BM+ stays focused

Vgate presents BM+ for BMW-oriented applications, and the live BimmerCode or BimmerLink selector controls the exact vehicle path. For an owner who has one supported BMW and no need for the extra networks documented for MX+, that narrower focus can be sufficient.

Preserve the exact BM+ revision and vendor firmware source. A similarly named reseller listing is not evidence that the received hardware follows the same support path.

## Approval, authenticity and updates

A marketplace logo is not an application approval. Check the BMW app selector, adapter manufacturer page and firmware route, then buy from a source with traceable model identity and return rights. Save those records with the vehicle and phone details.

This evidence packet matters when a future app update, phone change or vehicle purchase alters the compatibility chain. It also separates a product limitation from a counterfeit or stale-firmware problem.

## Use a garage scenario board

| Garage need | Better starting point | What still needs proof |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One supported BMW app | Either model | Exact vehicle, host and app approval |
| BMW plus Ford or GM networks | MX+ | Named network and application coverage |
| Lowest current price | Neither by default | Authentic seller and complete working system |
| Proven fastest or lowest drain | No documented winner | Controlled same-vehicle measurement |

The board deliberately leaves speed and drain unresolved because the cited vendor records do not provide a controlled head-to-head test.

## Do not rank what was not measured

Low-power and sleep behavior matter when an adapter remains connected, but figures published under different methods are not a battery-drain comparison. The same boundary applies to range, reconnect behavior and response speed.

Choose BM+ for a verified BMW-only application path when broader networks add no value. Choose MX+ when its additional hosts or documented Ford and GM networks support the actual garage. Recheck every app and vehicle before purchase.

## Compatibility evidence note

Platform, network and BMW-application claims were checked against the cited manufacturer documentation on August 29, 2026. Verify every vehicle, app, adapter revision and firmware path before purchase. No controlled speed, range or battery-drain comparison was performed.
