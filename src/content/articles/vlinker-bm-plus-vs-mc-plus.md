**Choose the vLinker BM+ when your job is centered on BimmerCode or BimmerLink and your exact BMW is listed by the app. Choose the MC+ when you also need the broader vehicle and HS/MS-CAN positioning Vgate documents, but verify the connection variant before ordering.** Neither name is a universal compatibility promise.

The two adapters look close on a product grid, but they answer different buying questions. BM+ is the narrower BMW-app choice. MC+ is the broader interface family. Your app, vehicle generation, phone or computer, and the exact Bluetooth or Wi-Fi variant decide whether either one is useful.

## BMW compatibility comes from the app

The current [BimmerCode adapter list](https://bimmercode.app/adapters/) includes both BM+ and MC+ across named BMW-family model groups. That is stronger evidence than a marketplace title, but it still is not permission to skip the vehicle selector. Model year, chassis generation, phone platform, app version and connection method all matter.

Use the [BimmerCode connection manual](https://bimmercode.app/manual/) as the operational source. Match four items before buying: exact car, exact app, exact adapter model and exact host platform. If one item is absent, treat compatibility as unresolved.

For BimmerLink, repeat the same app-specific check. An adapter working in one BMW app does not prove it works in every diagnostic or coding application. Our [BimmerLink adapter guide](/guides/bimmerlink-adapter/) explains that app-first verification sequence in more detail.

## Where the MC+ earns its extra scope

Vgate describes MC+ as supporting all legislated OBD-II protocols and highlights HS-CAN and MS-CAN coverage for vehicles such as Ford and GM. That is relevant if the adapter will serve more than a BMW household or an app that explicitly calls for those networks. It does not make MC+ automatically better for BimmerCode.

The MC+ listing also spans multiple wireless variants. Confirm the suffix and radio implementation in the actual checkout listing; do not assume that every MC+ unit exposes identical connection behavior to every operating system. For adjacent alternatives, compare [OBDLink CX with MC+](/guides/obdlink-cx-vs-vlinker-mc-plus/) or the [MX+ with BM+](/guides/obdlink-mx-plus-vs-vlinker-bm-plus/) using the same app-and-vehicle boundary.

## Price is a snapshot, not the decision

On September 1, 2026, Vgate's store showed BM+ at **$47.99 sale / $68 regular** and MC+ at **$66.99 sale / $69.99 regular**. Currency, tax, shipping, promotions and regional inventory can change. Recheck both official product pages at purchase time.

The small observed regular-price gap is not a reason to buy unused capability. Conversely, saving on BM+ is not useful if a second non-BMW workflow later requires the MC+ network coverage.

## A defensible buying sequence

1. Name the app and task: BimmerCode coding, BimmerLink diagnostics or another documented app.
2. Verify the exact vehicle and model year on the app's current compatibility page.
3. Verify the adapter by full model name, not just the vLinker brand.
4. For MC+, confirm the required Bluetooth or Wi-Fi variant and host operating system.
5. Recheck the official price, return terms and firmware support immediately before ordering.

## Bottom line

BM+ is the cleaner default for a verified BimmerCode/BimmerLink-only use case. MC+ is justified when an explicitly supported workflow needs its broader network or multi-brand scope. The correct adapter is the one named by the app for the specific car and host—not the one with the longer protocol list.
